# MediCore University Academic Regulations 2026

## [ATT-1.1] Purpose of Attendance Regulations

Attendance requirements are established to ensure that students participate
regularly in academic activities and remain engaged with their registered
courses. Attendance is recorded for lectures, tutorials, laboratory sessions,
seminars and other compulsory academic activities.

## [ATT-1.2] General Attendance Requirement

Every student enrolled in an undergraduate programme must maintain a minimum
attendance of 75% in each registered course during the semester.

Students whose attendance falls below the prescribed minimum may be declared
ineligible to appear for the corresponding end-semester examination.

Attendance is calculated using the total number of scheduled academic sessions
conducted during the semester.

## [ATT-1.3] Attendance Recording

Faculty members are responsible for recording attendance accurately for every
scheduled academic session.

Students are expected to verify their attendance records periodically through
the university's academic portal.

Any discrepancy in an attendance record must be reported to the concerned
faculty member within seven calendar days of the record being published.

## [ATT-1.4] Shortage of Attendance

A student whose attendance is below the required percentage shall normally be
required to compensate for the shortage through officially approved academic
activities where such activities are available.

Participation in optional events, student clubs, cultural activities or
informal study sessions does not automatically count toward course attendance.

## [ATT-1.5] Attendance and Examination Eligibility

Attendance eligibility is assessed before the commencement of the end-semester
examination period.

A student who does not satisfy the minimum attendance requirement may be
prevented from appearing for the examination in the affected course.

## [EXAM-2.1] Semester Examinations

End-semester examinations are conducted according to the academic calendar
published by the university.

Students must complete the examination registration process within the
prescribed period.

## [EXAM-2.2] Examination Registration

Only students who have completed examination registration and satisfy all
academic eligibility requirements may normally appear for an examination.

The examination office may withhold an examination admit card where a student
has outstanding administrative requirements.

## [EXAM-2.3] Examination Conduct

Students must arrive at the examination venue before the reporting time
specified on the examination schedule.

Students must carry their university identification card and examination
admit card unless an alternative arrangement has been formally communicated.

## [EXAM-2.4] Permitted Materials

Only materials specifically authorized by the examination office may be taken
into an examination room.

Electronic devices may not be used during an examination unless the
examination instructions explicitly authorize their use.

## [EXAM-2.5] Absence from Examination

A student who does not attend a scheduled examination will ordinarily be
recorded as absent.

Requests for consideration of examination absence must be submitted through
the procedure prescribed by the examination office.

## [EXAM-2.6] Medical Examination Absence

A student who is unable to attend an examination because of a documented
medical emergency may submit a medical request for consideration.

The request must be supported by documentation from a recognized medical
practitioner and submitted within the period specified by the examination
office.

Approval of a medical request does not automatically guarantee a replacement
examination.

## [MED-3.1] Medical Attendance Consideration

Students experiencing significant medical circumstances during a semester may
apply for attendance consideration.

The application must include appropriate medical documentation and must be
submitted through the designated academic office.

## [MED-3.2] Medical Attendance Relaxation

Where a student's medical circumstances are formally verified, the Academic
Review Committee may consider attendance relaxation.

A medically verified student may be considered eligible for an examination
with attendance as low as 65%, provided that the required medical documents
have been approved.

## [MED-3.3] Medical Documentation

Medical documents must clearly identify the period during which the student
was unable to participate in normal academic activities.

The university may request additional documentation where the submitted
material is insufficient to establish the circumstances described in the
application.

## [MED-3.4] Committee Review

Medical attendance requests are reviewed individually.

The committee may approve, partially approve or reject an application after
considering the submitted documentation and academic circumstances.

## [FEE-4.1] Semester Fees

Students must pay applicable semester fees within the payment period
announced by the university.

The applicable fee schedule is published through official university
communication channels.

## [FEE-4.2] Fee Payment Deadline

The standard deadline for payment of semester fees is the date specified in
the official fee schedule.

Students are responsible for checking the published schedule and completing
payment before the deadline.

## [FEE-4.3] Late Payment

A student who fails to pay fees by the standard deadline may be required to pay
a late payment charge.

Late payment may be accepted only during an officially announced late-payment
period.

## [FEE-4.4] Outstanding Fees

Students with outstanding fees may be restricted from completing certain
administrative processes until the outstanding amount is cleared.

The university may communicate an outstanding-fee notice before applying such
restrictions.

## [SCH-5.1] Scholarship Eligibility

University scholarships are subject to the eligibility criteria specified in
the applicable scholarship announcement.

Eligibility may depend on academic performance, financial circumstances,
attendance, disciplinary status or other criteria specified for a particular
scholarship.

## [SCH-5.2] Scholarship Applications

Students must submit scholarship applications before the deadline published
for the relevant scholarship.

Incomplete applications may not be considered.

## [SCH-5.3] Scholarship Verification

The university may verify information supplied in a scholarship application.

Students may be required to provide supporting documentation where necessary.

## [SCH-5.4] Scholarship Renewal

Scholarship renewal is not automatic.

A student seeking renewal must satisfy the renewal requirements published for
the relevant scholarship programme.

## [HOST-6.1] Hostel Residence

Students residing in university accommodation must comply with hostel rules,
safety requirements and instructions issued by authorized hostel personnel.

## [HOST-6.2] Hostel Entry

The normal hostel entry time for residents is restricted after 10:00 PM.

Students arriving after the prescribed time may be required to provide a valid
reason to the hostel security staff.

## [HOST-6.3] Hostel Visitors

Visitors may enter hostel premises only during the visiting hours announced by
the hostel administration.

Visitors must comply with identification and registration requirements.

## [HOST-6.4] Hostel Rooms

Students are responsible for maintaining reasonable cleanliness and order in
their assigned rooms.

University property must not be damaged, removed or altered without
authorization.

## [HOST-6.5] Hostel Leave

Students leaving the hostel overnight must complete the prescribed leave
procedure where required by hostel administration.

## [DISC-7.1] Student Conduct

Students are expected to behave respectfully toward other students, faculty,
staff and visitors.

Conduct that materially disrupts academic or residential activities may be
subject to disciplinary review.

## [DISC-7.2] Academic Integrity

Students must complete academic work honestly.

Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, fabrication of academic information
and other forms of academic misconduct may result in disciplinary action.

## [DISC-7.3] Disciplinary Procedure

A student accused of misconduct should receive notice of the allegation and an
opportunity to provide an explanation through the applicable disciplinary
procedure.

## [APPEAL-8.1] Academic Appeals

A student may submit an academic appeal where a university decision directly
affects the student's academic status.

Appeals must normally identify the decision being challenged and provide
relevant supporting information.

## [APPEAL-8.2] Appeal Review

Appeals are reviewed by the appropriate academic authority.

The reviewing authority may uphold, modify or refer a decision for further
consideration.

## [APPEAL-8.3] Committee Authority

The Academic Review Committee may consider exceptional academic circumstances
that are not adequately addressed by ordinary administrative procedures.

Where permitted by applicable university regulations, the committee may grant
a specific academic relaxation or waiver.

## [GRAD-9.1] Graduation Requirements

Students must satisfy the academic requirements of their degree programme
before graduation.

These requirements may include successful completion of required courses,
credits, projects, examinations and other programme requirements.

## [GRAD-9.2] Outstanding Requirements

A student who has not completed a required academic component may not normally
be awarded the degree until the requirement has been satisfied.

## [GEN-10.1] Interpretation of Regulations

University regulations should be interpreted together with applicable
departmental policies, examination instructions and formally approved
committee decisions.

Where a specific policy establishes a special procedure, that procedure may
apply to the circumstances covered by the policy.

## [GEN-10.2] Administrative Authority

Authorized university offices may issue procedural instructions necessary to
implement these regulations.

Such instructions should not be interpreted as changing substantive academic
requirements unless the issuing authority has the power to make such a
change.

## [GEN-10.3] Student Responsibility

Students are responsible for familiarizing themselves with regulations,
deadlines and procedures applicable to their programme and activities.

Failure to read a published regulation does not normally remove a student's
responsibility to comply with it.
