# MediCore University Examination Policy 2026

## EXAM-14.1 — Purpose

This policy establishes the procedures and standards governing semester examinations at MediCore University. It applies to undergraduate and postgraduate students, examination coordinators, faculty members, invigilators, and administrative personnel involved in the conduct of university examinations.

The purpose of the policy is to ensure that examinations are conducted fairly, securely, consistently, and in a manner that accurately measures student learning. The policy covers examination registration, eligibility, scheduling, examination halls, permitted materials, student identification, attendance, misconduct, absence, medical circumstances, evaluation, results, revaluation, and appeals.

Students are responsible for understanding the examination requirements that apply to their registered courses. Failure to follow examination procedures may result in administrative action or disciplinary review.

## EXAM-14.2 — Examination Schedule

The University shall publish the semester examination schedule through its official academic communication channels. The schedule normally includes the examination date, start time, duration, course code, course title, and examination venue.

Students should verify the schedule before each examination period. A student who identifies a timetable conflict must notify the academic administration office as soon as possible.

Once an examination schedule has been formally published, students are expected to appear according to the published schedule unless an approved exception applies.

## EXAM-14.3 — Examination Registration

Students must complete examination registration within the registration period announced by the University.

Registration may require confirmation of course enrollment, payment of applicable examination fees, and satisfaction of academic eligibility requirements.

A student who fails to complete registration within the prescribed period may be prevented from appearing for the examination unless an authorized late-registration procedure is available.

Late registration is not automatically granted. Requests may require approval from the academic administration office.

## EXAM-14.4 — Examination Eligibility

A student may appear for a semester examination when the student has satisfied the academic requirements applicable to the course and has completed examination registration.

Attendance requirements may affect examination eligibility. The general academic attendance requirement is governed by the University's attendance regulations.

Where an approved medical or exceptional attendance provision applies, examination eligibility shall be determined after the relevant documentation and approval have been considered.

## EXAM-14.5 — Student Identification

Students must carry valid University identification when appearing for an examination.

An invigilator may request identification before allowing a student to enter the examination hall. Students unable to establish their identity may be temporarily prevented from entering while the matter is verified.

Identification documents must not be altered or misrepresented.

## EXAM-14.6 — Examination Hall Entry

Students should arrive at the examination venue before the scheduled start time.

Students arriving after the examination has begun may be admitted at the discretion of the authorized examination personnel, subject to the examination rules and security requirements.

Late arrival does not normally result in additional examination time.

A student may be refused entry where late arrival creates a significant examination-security concern or where the examination administration has closed entry.

## EXAM-14.7 — Permitted Materials

Only materials specifically authorized for a particular examination may be brought into the examination hall.

Permitted materials may include writing instruments, approved calculators, formula sheets, or other materials expressly listed in the examination instructions.

A material that is permitted in one examination is not automatically permitted in another examination.

Students must review the instructions provided for each examination before entering the examination hall.

## EXAM-14.8 — Electronic Devices

Mobile phones, smart watches, wireless earphones, tablets, and other unauthorized electronic communication devices must not be used during an examination.

Where examination instructions require electronic devices to be deposited at a designated location, students must follow those instructions.

Possession of an unauthorized electronic device may be treated as an examination-rule violation even when the student claims that the device was not actively used.

## EXAM-14.9 — Examination Conduct

Students must maintain appropriate conduct throughout the examination.

Students must not communicate with other candidates except when communication is expressly permitted by the examination instructions or an invigilator.

Students must follow reasonable instructions issued by examination personnel.

Disruptive conduct, unauthorized communication, interference with another student's work, or refusal to follow examination instructions may result in removal from the examination hall and disciplinary review.

## EXAM-14.10 — Academic Misconduct

Academic misconduct during an examination includes, but is not limited to, unauthorized communication, possession of prohibited materials, copying, impersonation, unauthorized assistance, manipulation of examination materials, or obtaining examination information through prohibited means.

Suspected misconduct should be documented by the invigilator or examination personnel.

The University shall provide the student with an opportunity to respond to allegations according to applicable disciplinary procedures.

A finding of misconduct may result in cancellation of an examination attempt, academic penalties, or further disciplinary action.

## EXAM-14.11 — Examination Paper Security

Examination papers and other confidential examination materials must be handled according to University security procedures.

Students must not photograph, copy, distribute, or otherwise reproduce examination papers without authorization.

Examination personnel must take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized disclosure of examination materials before and during examinations.

## EXAM-14.12 — Answer Sheet Requirements

Students must use the answer sheets or examination systems specified for the examination.

Where required, students must write their registration number, course information, and other identifying information in the designated fields.

Students must not intentionally include identifying information in locations where anonymous evaluation is required.

Answer sheets must be submitted according to the instructions provided by examination personnel.

## EXAM-14.13 — Absence From Examination

A student who does not attend a scheduled examination without an approved reason may be recorded as absent.

Absence does not automatically create a right to a supplementary examination.

Students who miss an examination because of circumstances recognized under University policy may submit a formal request for consideration.

Requests should normally be submitted promptly and supported by appropriate documentation.

## EXAM-14.14 — Medical Examination Absence

A student unable to attend an examination because of a significant medical circumstance may request academic consideration.

The request should normally include documentation from a recognized medical professional or institution.

The University may verify submitted documentation where necessary.

Approval of a medical request does not guarantee a particular examination outcome. The academic administration office shall determine the appropriate academic arrangement according to applicable procedures.

## EXAM-14.15 — Emergency Circumstances

The University may consider examination requests involving serious and unexpected circumstances such as hospitalization, major accidents, or other verified emergencies.

Routine inconvenience, travel difficulties, personal scheduling conflicts, or failure to check the published examination schedule normally do not constitute sufficient grounds for special examination arrangements.

The decision shall consider the evidence provided and the academic impact of the circumstance.

## EXAM-14.16 — Examination Duration

The examination duration shall be specified in the examination instructions.

Students must stop writing or submitting responses when the examination period ends.

Students who continue working after the official end time may be subject to an examination-rule violation.

Invigilators shall provide reasonable warnings regarding the approaching end of the examination where operationally feasible.

## EXAM-14.17 — Leaving the Examination Hall

Students normally should remain in the examination hall until the minimum departure period specified by examination personnel has elapsed.

A student requiring an emergency departure should inform an invigilator.

Students must submit examination materials before leaving where required.

A student who leaves the examination hall may not normally return unless specifically authorized.

## EXAM-14.18 — Evaluation of Examinations

Examination responses shall be evaluated according to the approved assessment scheme for the relevant course.

Faculty members and authorized evaluators shall apply academic assessment criteria consistently.

Evaluation should be based on the student's submitted work and the approved marking requirements.

The University may conduct moderation or quality checks where required.

## EXAM-14.19 — Examination Results

Examination results shall be published through authorized University systems.

Students are responsible for reviewing their results and reporting apparent administrative errors within the applicable review period.

Publication of results does not prevent correction of verified administrative or calculation errors.

## EXAM-14.20 — Revaluation and Review

A student who believes that an examination result requires formal review may submit a revaluation or review request when the applicable process is available.

The request must be submitted within the announced period and may require payment of an administrative fee.

Revaluation shall be conducted according to the University's approved procedure.

A revaluation may result in the original marks being confirmed, increased, or changed according to the applicable rules.

## EXAM-14.21 — Result Correction

Administrative errors identified after result publication may be corrected following verification.

Examples include calculation errors, data-entry errors, incorrect course mapping, or other demonstrable administrative mistakes.

The University shall maintain an appropriate record of verified corrections.

## EXAM-14.22 — Supplementary Examination

Where University regulations provide for supplementary examinations, eligibility shall be determined according to the applicable academic rules.

A supplementary examination is not automatically available to every student who fails or misses an examination.

The University shall publish eligibility conditions, registration requirements, fees, and examination schedules where supplementary examinations are offered.

## EXAM-14.23 — Examination Appeals

Students may submit an academic appeal when they believe that an examination decision has been made incorrectly or that an approved procedure was not followed.

Appeals must identify the decision being challenged and provide relevant supporting information.

An appeal should be submitted within the prescribed period.

The appeal process does not guarantee a change in academic results.

## EXAM-14.24 — Examination Committee

The Examination Committee may review examination-related matters requiring institutional consideration.

The committee may consider cases involving examination misconduct, special circumstances, procedural disputes, examination administration, and other matters referred to it.

The committee may request additional information from students, faculty members, invigilators, or administrative offices.

## EXAM-14.25 — Confidentiality

Examination records, student submissions, misconduct reports, and review decisions should be handled confidentially and accessed only by authorized personnel.

Information may be shared when required for legitimate academic administration, disciplinary proceedings, legal obligations, or quality assurance.

## EXAM-14.26 — Accessibility Arrangements

Students requiring approved accessibility arrangements should contact the appropriate University office before the examination period whenever reasonably possible.

Approved arrangements may include additional time, accessible examination facilities, assistive technology, or other reasonable measures.

Accessibility arrangements must be formally approved and communicated to examination personnel.

## EXAM-14.27 — Technical Failure During Digital Examination

Where an examination is conducted digitally, students must report technical problems promptly through the designated examination support channel.

The University may verify technical records, submission logs, or system information when assessing a technical incident.

Where a verified technical failure materially affects an examination, the University may provide an appropriate remedy according to examination procedures.

## EXAM-14.28 — Examination Records

The University shall maintain examination records including registration information, attendance records, submitted results, approved review requests, and other records required for academic administration.

Records shall be retained according to applicable University record-management requirements.

## EXAM-14.29 — Student Responsibility

Students are responsible for checking examination schedules, completing registration, carrying required identification, following examination instructions, and complying with academic integrity requirements.

Failure to understand a published requirement does not normally exempt a student from complying with it.

Students should seek clarification from the appropriate academic office when a requirement is unclear.

## EXAM-14.30 — Interpretation

Questions regarding interpretation of this examination policy shall be referred to the authorized academic administration office.

Where another University regulation contains a more specific requirement, the more specific provision may apply to the relevant situation.

The University may review and amend examination procedures when necessary to improve academic administration, security, accessibility, or fairness.

**End of Examination Policy**
