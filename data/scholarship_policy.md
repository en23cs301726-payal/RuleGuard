# MediCore University Scholarship and Financial Assistance Policy 2026

## [SCH-13.1] Purpose

The university may provide scholarships and financial assistance to eligible
students to support academic participation and reduce financial barriers.

Scholarships may be funded by the university, external organizations,
alumni, charitable foundations or other approved sources.

Each scholarship programme may have its own eligibility requirements and
application procedure.

## [SCH-13.2] Scholarship Categories

Scholarships may include merit-based scholarships, need-based assistance,
achievement awards, research scholarships, sports scholarships and other
categories approved by the university.

A student may apply only for scholarships for which the student satisfies the
published eligibility requirements.

## [SCH-13.3] Merit Scholarship

A merit scholarship may be awarded to students who achieve the academic
performance specified in the relevant scholarship announcement.

Academic performance may be evaluated using semester grades, cumulative
academic performance or another officially specified measure.

## [SCH-13.4] Financial Assistance

Need-based financial assistance may be provided to students experiencing
documented financial difficulties.

Applicants may be required to provide information concerning household
income, educational expenses and other relevant financial circumstances.

The university may verify information supplied by an applicant.

## [SCH-13.5] Scholarship Application

Students must submit scholarship applications using the procedure specified in
the applicable scholarship announcement.

Applications submitted after the published deadline may normally be rejected.

## [SCH-13.6] Required Documents

A scholarship application may require academic records, identification,
income documentation, bank information or other supporting material.

The required documents shall be identified in the relevant scholarship
announcement.

Students are responsible for ensuring that submitted documents are accurate
and complete.

## [SCH-13.7] Incomplete Applications

The scholarship office may contact an applicant when a required document is
missing or unclear.

The applicant may be given an opportunity to provide the missing information
within the period specified by the scholarship office.

An incomplete application may be rejected where the applicant fails to provide
the requested information within the applicable period.

## [SCH-13.8] Scholarship Selection

Scholarship decisions may consider academic performance, financial
circumstances, extracurricular achievement, leadership, community
participation and other published criteria.

The selection process should be applied consistently to applicants within the
same scholarship category.

## [SCH-13.9] Scholarship Committee

A Scholarship Committee may review eligible applications and recommend
recipients.

The committee may request clarification from applicants where necessary.

The committee's decision shall be recorded through the applicable university
procedure.

## [SCH-13.10] Scholarship Award

A scholarship award shall specify the amount or benefit provided and the
period for which the award applies.

An award for one semester does not automatically establish entitlement to the
same award in subsequent semesters.

## [SCH-13.11] Scholarship Renewal

Renewal may depend on continued academic performance, attendance, disciplinary
standing and any additional criteria published for the scholarship.

Students must complete any required renewal process before the specified
deadline.

## [SCH-13.12] Attendance and Scholarships

Where attendance is listed as an eligibility requirement, the scholarship
office may use official attendance records supplied by the academic
administration.

Attendance requirements for scholarships are separate from general
examination eligibility unless the applicable scholarship rules expressly
state otherwise.

## [SCH-13.13] Scholarship Suspension

A scholarship may be suspended where a student fails to satisfy continuing
eligibility requirements.

The student should ordinarily be informed of the reason for suspension.

## [SCH-13.14] Scholarship Cancellation

A scholarship may be cancelled if it is established that the student
knowingly supplied false information or failed to meet a mandatory eligibility
condition.

Cancellation decisions should follow the applicable review procedure.

## [SCH-13.15] Change in Financial Circumstances

Students receiving need-based assistance should notify the scholarship office
if a material change occurs in the circumstances on which the award was based.

The university may review the continued eligibility of the student.

## [SCH-13.16] Multiple Scholarships

A student may not automatically combine multiple university-funded
scholarships.

Where a scholarship announcement contains restrictions on combining awards,
those restrictions apply to the relevant scholarship.

Students should disclose other scholarships when requested.

## [SCH-13.17] External Scholarships

Students may apply for scholarships provided by external organizations.

External scholarships are governed by the rules of the organization providing
the award.

The university may require students to notify the scholarship office when an
external scholarship affects university financial assistance.

## [SCH-13.18] Scholarship Payment

Scholarship benefits may be credited toward tuition fees, deposited into a
registered student account or paid through another officially approved
mechanism.

The payment method depends on the scholarship programme.

## [SCH-13.19] Scholarship Appeals

An applicant who believes that a scholarship decision was made incorrectly
may submit an appeal within the period specified by the scholarship office.

An appeal should identify the decision being challenged and provide relevant
supporting information.

## [SCH-13.20] Appeal Review

Scholarship appeals may be reviewed by an authority separate from the initial
application reviewer where required by university procedure.

The reviewing authority may uphold the original decision, modify it where
authorized or refer the matter for further review.

## [SCH-13.21] Academic Misconduct

A student found responsible for serious academic misconduct may become
ineligible for certain scholarship programmes.

The consequences depend on the requirements of the relevant scholarship.

## [SCH-13.22] Disciplinary Status

Scholarship eligibility may be affected by disciplinary action where the
relevant scholarship rules identify disciplinary standing as an eligibility
condition.

A pending allegation does not automatically establish that a student has
violated a disciplinary requirement.

## [SCH-13.23] Scholarship Records

Scholarship applications and decisions should be maintained according to
university record-management requirements.

Access to financial information should be restricted to authorized personnel.

## [SCH-13.24] Privacy

Information supplied for financial assistance should be used only for
legitimate scholarship administration, verification and related university
purposes.

The university should take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized
disclosure.

## [SCH-13.25] Scholarship Communication

Scholarship deadlines, eligibility requirements and application instructions
may be communicated through the student portal, official email or other
designated university channels.

Students are responsible for checking official communication concerning
scholarships.

## [SCH-13.26] Exceptional Scholarship Cases

Exceptional scholarship cases may be referred to the Scholarship Committee
when circumstances are not adequately addressed by the ordinary scholarship
procedure.

The committee may recommend an appropriate solution within its authority.

## [SCH-13.27] Scholarship Funding

Scholarship awards are subject to the availability of funds allocated to the
relevant scholarship programme.

The publication of a scholarship opportunity does not guarantee that every
eligible applicant will receive an award.

## [SCH-13.28] End of Scholarship

A scholarship normally ends when the award period expires, the student no
longer satisfies the eligibility requirements or the student's programme
status changes in a way that makes the award inapplicable.

The scholarship office may communicate the end of an award to the student.

## [SCH-13.29] Student Responsibility

Students are responsible for reading the scholarship conditions applicable to
their award.

Students should promptly communicate material changes that could affect their
eligibility.

## [SCH-13.30] Interpretation

Where a scholarship-specific rule differs from a general financial assistance
rule, the scholarship-specific provision should be considered for students
covered by that programme.

Questions concerning interpretation may be referred to the Scholarship Office.
