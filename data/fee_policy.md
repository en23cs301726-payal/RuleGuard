# MediCore University Fee and Financial Policy 2026

## [FEE-11.1] Fee Schedule

All students enrolled in an academic programme are required to pay the applicable
tuition and institutional fees for each semester.

The university publishes the fee schedule before the beginning of the relevant
payment period.

The fee schedule shall identify the amount payable, payment period and standard
payment deadline.

Students are responsible for checking the official fee schedule and ensuring
that payments are completed within the applicable period.

## [FEE-11.2] Standard Payment Deadline

The standard deadline for semester fee payment shall be the date published in
the official fee schedule.

Unless a separate approved arrangement applies, students are expected to
complete payment by the published deadline.

A payment made after the standard deadline may result in an additional late
payment charge.

## [FEE-11.3] Late Payment Period

The university may establish a late-payment period following the standard
payment deadline.

Where a late-payment period is officially announced, students may complete
their payment during that period subject to the applicable late-payment charge.

The existence of a late-payment period does not create a permanent right to
pay fees after the standard deadline.

## [FEE-11.4] Administrative Restrictions

A student with outstanding fees may be restricted from completing specified
administrative activities.

Such activities may include examination registration, receipt of official
documents or other processes identified by the university.

Restrictions should be communicated to affected students through an official
communication channel.

## [FEE-11.5] Emergency Financial Circumstances

Students experiencing documented financial hardship may submit a request for
additional time to pay their semester fees.

The request must be submitted before the standard payment deadline wherever
reasonably possible.

Submission of a request does not by itself extend the payment deadline.

## [FEE-11.6] Payment Extension

An approved payment extension may allow a student to complete payment after the
standard deadline without being treated as an ordinary late payment.

The extension must be formally approved by an authorized financial officer or
committee.

The approval should specify the final date by which payment must be completed.

## [FEE-11.7] Fee Refunds

Refund requests must be submitted according to the applicable refund schedule.

The amount refunded, if any, may depend on the date of withdrawal and the
reason for withdrawal.

Administrative charges may be deducted where permitted by the applicable
refund policy.

## [FEE-11.8] Refund Processing

Approved refunds are normally processed through the payment method or bank
details registered with the university.

Students may be asked to verify their payment information before a refund is
processed.

## [FEE-11.9] Financial Assistance

Students may apply for financial assistance programmes announced by the
university.

Financial assistance may be subject to academic, financial and administrative
eligibility criteria.

Receiving financial assistance does not automatically remove the student's
responsibility to satisfy applicable fee requirements.

## [FEE-11.10] Fee Disputes

A student who believes that an amount has been incorrectly charged may submit a
fee dispute to the finance office.

The dispute should include the student's identification details, the disputed
amount and supporting documentation.

## [FEE-11.11] Fee Dispute During Registration

A pending fee dispute does not automatically prevent the university from
applying ordinary registration deadlines.

Students should contact the finance office promptly when a disputed amount
could affect registration.

## [FEE-11.12] Committee Review of Financial Cases

Exceptional financial cases may be referred to the Financial Review Committee.

The committee may consider documented circumstances and may recommend an
extension, installment arrangement or other permitted relief.

## [FEE-11.13] Installment Arrangements

Where permitted, students may request payment through an approved installment
arrangement.

An installment arrangement must identify the number of installments and the
date on which each installment becomes due.

Failure to comply with an approved installment arrangement may result in
withdrawal of the arrangement.

## [FEE-11.14] Payment Confirmation

Students should retain electronic or physical evidence of completed payments
until the payment has been reflected in the university's financial system.

A transaction receipt alone does not guarantee that a payment has been
successfully credited if the transaction is subsequently reversed.

## [FEE-11.15] Communication of Deadlines

Fee deadlines may be communicated through the student portal, official email,
published notices or other officially designated channels.

Students are expected to monitor these channels during the payment period.

## [FEE-11.16] Exceptional Deadline Provision

In exceptional circumstances, the Finance Office may authorize a student to
complete payment up to **20 August** even when the standard semester fee
deadline has passed.

Such approval requires a documented reason and confirmation from the
authorized officer.

## [FEE-11.17] Standard Academic Deadline

For the 2026 academic session, semester fees must ordinarily be paid no later
than **15 August**.

Payments completed after this date are considered late unless an approved
exception or extension applies.

## [FEE-11.18] Relationship With Other Policies

This policy should be read together with the academic regulations,
scholarship policy and other financial instructions issued by the university.

Where a specific financial provision applies to a particular category of
student, that provision may govern the circumstances covered by it.
