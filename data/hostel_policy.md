# MediCore University Hostel Policy 2026

## [HOST-12.1] Purpose of Hostel Regulations

The university hostel provides residential accommodation for eligible students
and aims to maintain a safe, orderly and supportive living environment.

All residents must comply with hostel regulations, safety procedures and
reasonable instructions issued by authorized hostel personnel.

## [HOST-12.2] Eligibility for Hostel Accommodation

Hostel accommodation may be provided to students who satisfy the eligibility
criteria published by the university.

Allocation may depend on availability, programme requirements, distance from
the university, student category and other criteria established by the hostel
administration.

A hostel room is allocated to a specific student and may not ordinarily be
transferred to another person without authorization.

## [HOST-12.3] Room Allocation

The hostel administration determines room allocation.

Students may submit requests for room changes where a genuine academic,
medical, safety or administrative reason exists.

A request for a room change does not guarantee that another room will be
available.

## [HOST-12.4] Normal Hostel Curfew

Residents are expected to return to the hostel by **10:00 PM** on ordinary
academic days.

After 10:00 PM, entry may be restricted and residents arriving after the
curfew may be asked to provide a reason for their late arrival.

Repeated violations may be referred to the hostel administration.

## [HOST-12.5] Late Entry Permission

A student who expects to arrive after the normal curfew may request permission
for late entry through the hostel administration.

Permission should ordinarily be requested before the student leaves the
campus or residence area.

In emergencies, permission may be requested after the student has arrived.

## [HOST-12.6] Academic Activities After Curfew

Students participating in officially authorized university activities may be
permitted to return after the normal hostel curfew.

The activity should be organized or recognized by an authorized university
department, faculty member or student affairs office.

Students should carry evidence of participation when requested by hostel
security.

## [HOST-12.7] Hostel Security

Security personnel may verify the identity of residents entering hostel
premises.

Students must cooperate with reasonable security checks.

Security personnel should maintain records of unusual or exceptional late
entries where required for safety purposes.

## [HOST-12.8] Visitor Policy

Visitors may enter hostel premises only during approved visiting hours.

Visitors must register at the designated security point.

Residents are responsible for the conduct of their visitors while they are on
hostel premises.

## [HOST-12.9] Overnight Visitors

Visitors may not ordinarily stay overnight in student accommodation.

An exception may be considered where written permission has been granted by
the hostel administration.

Unauthorized overnight stays may be treated as a hostel rule violation.

## [HOST-12.10] Room Cleanliness

Residents must maintain reasonable cleanliness in their rooms.

Food waste, packaging and other waste should be disposed of using designated
waste facilities.

Students may be required to clean areas affected by activities originating
from their room.

## [HOST-12.11] Hostel Property

Furniture, electrical equipment and other hostel property must not be removed
from assigned rooms or common areas without authorization.

Students may be held responsible for damage caused by intentional misuse or
negligence.

## [HOST-12.12] Electrical Safety

Students must not use electrical equipment that creates an unreasonable fire
or electrical hazard.

High-power appliances may be restricted in accordance with hostel safety
instructions.

Students should report damaged electrical outlets, exposed wiring or other
hazards immediately.

## [HOST-12.13] Prohibited Activities

Residents must not engage in activities that create a significant safety
risk, disturb other residents or damage university property.

The hostel administration may issue additional reasonable safety instructions
where circumstances require them.

## [HOST-12.14] Quiet Hours

Quiet hours normally begin at **10:30 PM** and continue until **6:30 AM**.

During quiet hours, residents should avoid loud music, shouting and other
unnecessary noise.

Academic discussion conducted at a reasonable volume is permitted unless it
disturbs other residents.

## [HOST-12.15] Hostel Leave

Students intending to remain away from the hostel overnight should complete
the applicable hostel leave procedure.

The procedure may require the student to provide the destination, expected
return date and an emergency contact.

## [HOST-12.16] Emergency Leave

In an emergency, a student may leave the hostel before completing the normal
leave procedure.

The student should inform the hostel administration as soon as reasonably
possible.

## [HOST-12.17] Weekend Travel

Students may travel outside the university campus during weekends provided
that their academic and hostel responsibilities are not affected.

Students remaining away overnight should follow the applicable leave
procedure.

## [HOST-12.18] Curfew Exception for Registered Activities

For the purpose of this policy, a student participating in a **registered
university activity may return to the hostel until 11:00 PM** without obtaining
individual late-entry permission.

The activity must be listed or approved by the relevant university authority.

The student may be required to show the activity schedule or other reasonable
evidence of participation.

## [HOST-12.19] Ordinary Curfew

Except where an approved exception applies, students must return to the hostel
by **10:00 PM**.

A student who has not received an applicable exception and arrives after
10:00 PM may be subject to the normal late-entry procedure.

## [HOST-12.20] Curfew and Academic Responsibility

Permission to return after curfew does not excuse a student from attending
classes, completing assignments or following other academic requirements.

Students remain responsible for managing their schedules appropriately.

## [HOST-12.21] Health and Safety Exceptions

A student facing an immediate health or safety concern should seek appropriate
assistance rather than attempting to comply with ordinary hostel timing
requirements.

Emergency circumstances should be reported to hostel administration as soon
as reasonably possible.

## [HOST-12.22] Disciplinary Review

Repeated or serious hostel violations may be referred to the appropriate
disciplinary authority.

The student should ordinarily be informed of the allegation and given an
opportunity to provide an explanation.

## [HOST-12.23] Hostel Complaints

Residents may submit complaints concerning accommodation, security, cleanliness
or other hostel matters through the designated hostel administration channel.

Complaints should contain sufficient information for the administration to
investigate the matter.

## [HOST-12.24] Interpretation

Hostel regulations should be interpreted together with university safety
policies and authorized instructions issued by the student affairs office.

Where an approved exception specifically applies to a student's circumstances,
the exception should be considered together with the ordinary hostel rule.

## [HOST-12.25] Responsibility of Residents

Every resident is responsible for becoming familiar with the hostel rules.

Failure to read a published hostel regulation does not normally exempt a
resident from compliance.

## [HOST-12.26] Review of Hostel Rules

The university may periodically review hostel regulations to reflect changes
in accommodation arrangements, safety requirements or student needs.

Changes should be communicated through an officially designated channel.
