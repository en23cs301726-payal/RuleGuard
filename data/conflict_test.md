# RuleGuard — Planted Contradictions

## [ATT-CONFLICT-1] General Attendance Requirement

Every undergraduate student must maintain a minimum attendance of 60% in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

## [HOST-CONFLICT-1] Hostel Entry Time

The normal hostel entry time for residents is restricted after 11:00 PM. Students arriving after this time may be required to provide a valid reason to hostel security staff.

## [MED-CONFLICT-1] Medical Attendance Relaxation

Where a student's medical circumstances are formally verified, the Academic Review Committee may consider the student eligible for an examination with attendance as low as 60%, provided that the required medical documents have been approved.
